About the Movie

Kaho Naa... Pyaar Hai is a 2000 Indian Hindi-language romantic musical film directed by Rakesh Roshan. The film stars Hrithik Roshan and Amisha Patel in lead roles, with Anupam Kher, Dalip Tahil, and others in supporting roles.

The story revolves around a young man who falls in love with a woman, but their love faces challenges due to circumstances beyond their control.

Genre: Romantic, Musical

Release Date: 14 January 2000

Language: Hindi

This movie was Hrithik Roshan's debut film and was a major commercial success. release may vary by region

Movie Story

story summary

The film follows the story of Rohit, a young man who falls in love with Sonia. However, their love faces obstacles when Rohit is tragically killed. Sonia is devastated but later discovers that Rohit has a twin brother, Raj, who helps her cope with her loss and brings hope back into her life.

Did You Know?

The film was a major breakthrough for Hrithik Roshan, who won the Filmfare Award for Best Actor for his performance. It also marked the debut of Amisha Patel, who received critical acclaim for her role.
